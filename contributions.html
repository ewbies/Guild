Note: Per-hour rates are calculated from per-minute rates (change per minute × 60). Rates are based on the difference between current and previous contribution totals divided by time elapsed in minutes. Rates are smoothed using exponential moving average (EMA) to reduce volatility from short time intervals and bursty activity patterns. Short intervals (< 5 min) use stronger smoothing for stability, while longer intervals use lighter smoothing for faster response. On first load, rates will show "---" until we have at least 2 data points. If no change is detected on refresh, the last calculated rate is preserved to avoid showing 0/hr. Auto-refresh updates the page every 15 seconds to show the latest contribution data. Elemental shard rates use a rolling 6-hour window based on individual drop events (not total values). Shard rates have a minimum 1-hour duration to prevent inflated rates from short activity bursts. Collective shard rates sum all individual member rates, not guild fund changes.
